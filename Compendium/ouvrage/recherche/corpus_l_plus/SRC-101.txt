Agentic AI Market Analysis by Mordor Intelligence

The Agentic AI market size is expected to grow from USD 9.89 billion in 2026 to USD 14.06 billion in 2027 and is forecast to reach USD 82.27 billion by 2032 at 42.38% CAGR over 2027-2032.

Agent development and deployment in the United States create an implementation pipeline, and customer-service applications connect agent software to recurring operational work. Managed runtimes lower the work of operating agents, but skills and trust constrain the scope of tasks buyers can delegate. Suppliers can respond by combining workflow integration with governance controls, linking commercial value to reliable execution rather than the number of available agents. Vendors that make delegated actions governable can therefore compete for a wider set of customer-service tasks, while agent availability alone leaves the trust constraint unresolved.

Surge in demand for intelligent automation

Buyers seeking intelligent automation create demand for software that can repeat a useful task across many agent instances. In IBM's January-April 2026 survey, technology executives anticipated a 38% increase in deployed AI agents by 2027. The survey covered 2,000 senior executives responsible for IT, technology, or AI decisions across 33 geographies and 19 industries. An expanded agent population would increase the opportunity to supply software that carries a defined process through successive executions. Where the same task recurs, a supplier can separate reusable application logic from the configuration needed for individual agent instances. That division can support software licensing across deployments, with implementation services concentrated on work that cannot be standardized.

Proliferation of multi-agent systems for complex problem-solving

Organizations developing multiple agents must decide how those agents contribute to one business objective and where coordination belongs within the software. KPMG’s Q3 2026 US survey found that 25% of represented organizations were actively developing or implementing multi-agent systems, up from 6% in the prior quarter.[1] The survey covered 314 US executives and business leaders at organizations with annual revenue of at least USD 1 billion, surveyed from July 24 through August 25, 2026. As several agents contribute to one objective, coordinating their work becomes part of the value of the overall solution. A buyer commissioning several agents must also resolve which component carries each task and how responsibility passes between them. Suppliers that package coordination with implementation are therefore positioned to meet that growth in development activity.

Advances in cloud-native AI infrastructure

Managed cloud runtimes expand the addressable software opportunity by reducing the infrastructure work required to operate agents beyond a demonstration. AWS made its Amazon Bedrock AgentCore managed harness generally available on June 17, 2026, in AWS Commercial Regions where AgentCore is available.[2] The harness handles orchestration, tool execution, persistent session state, recovery from failures, and session isolation. Microsoft Foundry Hosted Agents are also generally available, with Microsoft operating containers, scaling, session lifecycle, and platform integration. These services let application suppliers concentrate more engineering effort on connecting agents to useful business actions rather than recreating execution infrastructure. For agent application vendors, the sale can center on workflow-specific software while the cloud operator supplies managed execution infrastructure.

Integration with spatial-computing/XR ecosystems

Spatial-computing integration can create demand when an agent must act on the object or task an employee is viewing. On September 16, 2026, Snap announced Salesforce was bringing Agentforce to SPECS AR glasses, where employees could identify objects, create issue cases, retrieve information, and update workflows. For a maintenance worker, the announced link from that view to an issue record would make the agent part of the job rather than a separate information tool. The commercial product is the connection between visual context and a business action, with software carrying the task across interfaces. That connection would require context recognition, a relevant workflow, and a bounded action before it could justify an organizational subscription. Adoption therefore depends on solving a complete work step, making application integration more consequential than adding an agent to a display.

Organizational change-management and skill gaps

The ability to build an agent does not equip an organization to redesign the work around it. Harvard Business Review Analytic Services' July 2025 survey found that 48% of 623 respondents cited a lack of talent or skills as an agentic AI adoption barrier. Respondents were HBR audience members involved in data and technology decisions at organizations using or considering agentic AI. In the same survey, 31% cited difficulties adjusting work processes to accommodate agentic AI. These barriers can lengthen the path from a software decision to usable workflows, even when the agent itself functions. Longer implementation cycles can tie up supplier delivery capacity, limiting how quickly additional agent projects can become operating software.

Ethical, bias and transparency concerns

Ethical concerns constrain demand when buyers must defend the consequences of delegating decisions to agents. Capgemini Research Institute's July 2025 report identified trust and safety as among the most significant barriers to widespread adoption of AI agents.[3] Its April 2025 research surveyed 1,500 director-level or higher executives across 14 countries at organizations with more than USD 1 billion in annual revenue, all exploring agentic AI. A functioning agent can therefore leave a buyer unwilling to authorize consequential tasks without additional assurance about its decisions. Unresolved trust restricts the breadth of workflows vendors can convert into paid deployments, creating an adoption constraint even where technical capability is available.

Agentic AI Market Segment Analysis

By Component:
Recurring Software Supports Solution Scale

The solution segment accounted for a 61.21% share of the Agentic AI market size in 2026, making it the largest component segment. Salesforce's Agentforce annual recurring revenue exceeded USD 1.5 billion in Q2 FY2027, ended July 31, 2026, including Slackbot and Headless 360 AI offerings. Agentforce and Slack delivered 3.2 billion Agentic Work Units during the quarter, up 97% quarter over quarter. For solution providers, repeated execution can sustain an ongoing revenue relationship after the initial deployment, making service reliability and useful workflow coverage central to the software offering.

Services are the fastest-growing segment, forecast at 44% CAGR over 2027-2032. In March 2026, SEI said it would work with IBM Consulting on enterprise transformation through agentic AI and automation. The engagement called for a data-driven review of operational systems and workflows to advance process redesign and modernization. Such work can translate an organization's existing operating model into specific agent tasks and review points, creating implementation and customization opportunities around the software. Service vendors can therefore earn from the process changes needed before large-scale use, with commercial value in defining the work an agent platform must carry.

By Deployment Mode:
Cloud Scale and Hybrid Integration

Cloud represented a 59.83% Agentic AI market share in 2026, making it the largest deployment segment. IDC's AWS-commissioned 2026 custom survey of more than 900 organizations across 15 industries and 10 countries found that 63% tested and deployed agents in public cloud infrastructure. Operating an agent as a cloud-delivered service can bring repeated interactions onto the same application, placing continuous execution at the center of the offering. A provider operating that application can handle operational changes centrally, without a separate implementation for each user interaction. That makes service operation a commercial capability in its own right, with value extending beyond the initial configuration of an agent.

Hybrid is the fastest-growing deployment segment, forecast at 44.85% CAGR over 2027-2032. In August 2026, AWS reported that Mobileye operated its production AI Support Agent on AgentCore's serverless runtime, with an on-premises ticket-management system supplying tickets and receiving AI-generated responses. Growth can come from implementing that connection as a governed workflow, with clear data access and a usable return path for completed actions. On-premises deployment plays a distinct role in regulated industries, where 63% of 413 AI practitioners and leaders surveyed by DataRobot in 2026 said their organizations required it for agentic AI. Hybrid creates an integration opportunity around retaining a local workflow while adding cloud agent execution, rather than making application replacement a prerequisite for automation.

By Organization Size:
Enterprise Deployment and SME Workflow Adoption

Large enterprises were the largest organization-size segment, with a 64.7% share in 2026. On February 10, 2026, DXC Technology announced completing an enterprise-wide Amazon Quick deployment covering its workforce of 115,000 employees across 70 countries. Amazon Quick was deployed as an agentic AI-powered digital workspace, making a common application the foundation of this organization-wide program. A rollout of that reach can create implementation and support work around making the same software usable across many locations. Such buyers create revenue potential through the reach of a common platform, so implementation scope can matter as much as the number of distinct agent applications.

Small and medium enterprises (SMEs) are the fastest-growing organization-size segment, forecast at 43.8% CAGR over 2027-2032. OpenAI's EU analysis defined small and medium businesses as those with 200 or fewer associated users. Weekly active users of Codex and ChatGPT Work in that group were 8.1 times their May 1-7, 2026 level in the week ending October 5, 2026. The increase identifies a larger active user base for those tools, creating room for services that connect them to a specific organizational task. Packaging that task into a manageable implementation and support offering can link growing tool use with a commercial service relationship.

By End-user Industry:
Financial Workflows and Automotive Procurement

BFSI was the largest end-user segment, with a 19.03% share in 2026. Cambridge's 2026 global financial-services survey found 23% of industry respondents, including fintechs, at scaling or transforming stages of agentic AI adoption. Capgemini's June-July 2025 survey of 1,100 financial-services leaders across 14 markets found that 75% of bank respondents prioritized customer service for AI-agent adoption at scale. Customer service provides a specific route into such programs because the application can be attached to an identifiable recurring operating process. The path to sector revenue therefore runs through defined workflows, giving specialized software a clearer commercial opening than an undirected platform experiment.

Automotive is the fastest-growing end-user segment, forecast at 45.35% CAGR over 2027-2032. BMW uses an agentic purchasing system to manage approximately 250,000 specialized tools used in component production and maintenance, including tools at supplier facilities. Its agents draft inventory requests, send them to suppliers, review responses, and approve uncomplicated cases, while specialists handle exceptions. Routine processing can scale through the software, but wider deployment can depend on specialist capacity for exceptions, so the automated purchasing workflow needs an explicit route for cases it cannot approve.

By Agent Architecture:
Coordination Reliability Shapes Design Choices

Multi-agent systems (MAS) were the largest architecture segment, with a 53.81% share in 2026, and are forecast to be the fastest-growing segment at 43.75% CAGR over 2027-2032. Druva's DruAI coordinates 8 to 10 specialized agents in production depending on the workflow, supporting cyber investigation, remediation, and recovery. Dividing a complex objective into separate agent tasks can allow specialized execution, but the workflow must preserve a common understanding of what has been completed. For the organizational buyer, this places value in the handoffs, checks, and task boundaries that connect independent components to one business result. A failed handoff can undermine the whole process even when individual agents perform their own tasks correctly, so buyers need reliability across the recovery workflow as well as within each component.

Building a larger agent network can add coordination choices, so a narrower task may justify a simpler architecture rather than more components. Single-agent systems were viewed by employees in a 2025 preliminary IT-support study as promising greater efficiency while risking reduced oversight. The trade-off suggests that the number of agents does not determine how work is checked. For bounded support tasks, a single execution path can simplify component coordination without resolving the perceived oversight risk. The commercial value of a simpler design therefore depends on preserving review and exception handling, rather than merely removing coordination between agents.

By Application:
Procurement Workflows and Customer Resolution

Autonomous process automation was the largest application segment, with a 23.03% share in 2026. IBM and StarLink announced an agentic procurement solution on September 10, 2026, projected to save StarLink 11,500 hours annually. StarLink, an Infinigate Group company, was the first user of the solution built with IBM Bob and IBM watsonx Orchestrate. The projection allocated 6,000 hours to 9,000 purchase orders, 1,500 hours to 14,000 quotes, and 4,000 hours to quote comparison. Because the projection spans purchase orders, quotes, and quote comparison, agent software here is valued for carrying a procurement process across several steps rather than producing one document.

Intelligent virtual assistants are the fastest-growing application segment, forecast at 44.45% CAGR over 2027-2032. By June 25, 2026, Salesforce's service agent had handled 4.3 million inquiries on its own help website and resolved 70% of them. That resolution measure gives a deployed assistant a commercial role beyond answer presentation, linking its value to the result achieved within a service workflow. A provider can design the application around the kinds of questions an agent can resolve, with unresolved work following a separately defined service path. Useful resolution gives a provider a concrete basis for expanding the assistant's role within support operations and related workflow services.

Geography Analysis

North America Agentic AI Market

North America held 39.95% of the Agentic AI market size in 2026, making it the largest regional market. KPMG's Q3 2026 US survey found 62% of represented organizations with annual revenue of at least USD 1 billion building, deploying, or developing AI agents, up from 53% in Q2. Development-stage work can support implementation services before an agent enters production, while deployment creates an operating-software relationship, giving suppliers distinct ways to engage those buyers. Because the survey combines building, deploying, and developing, suppliers that carry projects from development into production can extend their role beyond the initial software build.

Asia-Pacific Agentic AI Market

Asia-Pacific is the fastest-growing geography, forecast at 45.2% CAGR over 2027-2032. Bank of Singapore, OCBC's private-banking subsidiary, rolled out an agentic source-of-wealth tool in 2025. The tool extracted, structured, and synthesized customer records and supporting documents, and prompted follow-ups for information gaps. Relationship managers' average report-preparation time fell from 10 days to 1 hour, while relationship managers and compliance teams retained judgment and final approval. The growth mechanism is the integration of agent-led preparation into a regulated customer-review workflow, where separating document work from final approval can expand useful automation without transferring the decision itself.

Google Cloud's NVIDIA-powered G4 availability in São Paulo, Brazil, in September 2026 sits alongside Mibanco's October 2025 WhatsApp banking pilot in Peru, whose agents could open savings accounts after secret-key, facial-biometric, and identity-document checks. A 2026 report identified fragmented data, foreign technology dependence, and uneven national readiness as constraints on scaling agents in Europe. South Africa's Capitec operated agentic AI in business-banking credit processing during FY2026, ended February 28, 2026. The United Arab Emirates' Federal Authority for Identity, Citizenship, Customs & Port Security reported in August 2026 that it had formed agentic AI working teams and assigned responsibilities. Local compute availability and identity-checked consumer channels in South America therefore present different delivery requirements from Capitec's internal credit operation and the UAE authority's federal project.

Regulatory Framework and Outlook

This comparison considers selected jurisdictions from the perspective of organizational agentic software providers handling personal information or supplying covered generated-content services. Canada's federal privacy framework addresses consent for covered processing, while France's data-protection guidance explains roles and a legal basis for AI development using personal data. China's labeling measures concern covered synthetic-content services, while Brazil has named AI personal-data processing as a 2026-2027 supervisory priority. Regulatory work therefore attaches to the supplier's actual data and output uses, which determine the relevant compliance tasks.

These roles create different opportunities to earn software revenue and implementation fees as a functioning agent becomes part of a business process.

Competitive Landscape

Market Concentration

The competitive structure is moderately fragmented, with suppliers offering different combinations of agent development, data integration, simulation modeling, and workflow capabilities. Microsoft Corporation reported more than 17,000 customers using Foundry and Fabric together in FY2026 Q4, up 60% year over year, as enterprises connected agents to Fabric data. Google LLC supplies agent-platform software, and The AnyLogic Company supplies simulation-modeling software. Ampcome Technologies Pvt Ltd offers a conversational-code platform for businesses to build agents integrated with enterprise workflows. Amazon Web Services Inc. supplies AgentCore.

In October 2025, Amazon Web Services made AgentCore generally available in 9 AWS Regions spanning the United States, Europe, and Asia-Pacific. The platform provides a foundation to build, deploy, and operate agents, connecting software development with an operating runtime under one provider. Specialist vendors can build workflow-specific applications on that foundation rather than supply every lifecycle service themselves. The resulting division of responsibility gives buyers separate choices about business-task functionality and the infrastructure used to run it.

Managing agents from multiple developers creates a need for controls that span the buyer's deployed applications. Microsoft made Agent 365 generally available to commercial customers in May 2026 as a control plane for observing, governing, and securing agents. Its scope includes Microsoft-built and ecosystem-partner agents, using existing administrative and security workflows. This allows the buyer to organize oversight around existing administrative workflows instead of the origin of each agent. Governance can thus be bought at Microsoft's platform layer even for an ecosystem partner's application, giving Microsoft an administrative role without requiring it to build that agent.
