Read this before the ranking

A score measures how much an institution has published and launched , not whether its AI is good, fair or safe. Smaller institutions often use vendor AI they never announce, so a low score can mean “quiet”, not “behind”.

How points are earned

Deployment : live customer uses across six areas

Governance : principles, board oversight, risk disclosure, an accountable executive

Model risk : model-risk controls that cover AI, aligned to OSFI E-23

Transparency : customers told; voluntary code; regular reporting

Talent : AI lab, disclosed headcount, active hiring

How the Index is built
From a bank’s own words to a score you can check

Collect what institutions publish

Annual reports, filings, newsrooms, product pages, patents, job boards and reputable news: 210 AI uses at 27 institutions, each dated by when it launched and linked to its source.

Score five pillars, 20 points each

Customer deployment, governance, model-risk readiness, transparency and talent. Every point is a yes-or-no criterion met only with a public, dated source, and the same rules apply to every institution.

Flows: plain-language trading automation engine

Questrade announced Flows, which lets clients describe an investment strategy in plain language and then builds and runs the automation, including placing trades for them; Questrade presents it as part of its 'agentic finance' launch.

Context: The release does not name the AI model behind Flows; Fintech.ca (2026-07-29) describes it under 'AI-Powered Trading Automations'. Not marked 'coming soon' in the release, but rollout status not verified. 'Canada's first' is Questrade's claim.

Announced Jul 30, 2026 Questrade

Questrade MCP: client accounts connected to Claude and ChatGPT

Questrade announced an MCP integration that lets clients connect accounts to AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT to view and analyse holdings, research markets and initiate orders, which clients confirm in the Questrade app; Questrade calls itself the first Canadian institution to do this officially.

Context: Beta: NCFA Canada (https://ncfacanada.org/questrade-mcp-ai-brokerage-accounts/) reports Questrade published beta documentation on 2026-07-23 supporting Claude and Claude Code. 'First' is Questrade's claim. The AI models are third-party (the client's own assistant). In-app order confirmation per BetaKit 2026-07-30.

SmartDecision AI-enhanced life insurance underwriting

BMO Insurance launched SmartDecision, which uses predictive modelling and AI-enhanced capabilities to give real-time underwriting decisions on eligible life applications up to $5 million, often without medical requirements.

Context: Advisor-led application; eligible applications only.

Regulation tracker
The rules, and when they bite

Coming Federal (Canada)

OSFI Guideline E-23 (model risk)

Final, published September 11, 2025. Takes effect May 1, 2027, and expressly covers AI and machine-learning models.

E-23 effective for federally regulated financial institutions , May 1, 2027 : 181 days to go

Coming Quebec

AMF guideline on AI (Quebec)

Final. The guideline is dated March 2026, the AMF’s guideline page was updated April 24, 2026, and it takes effect May 1, 2027 for institutions the AMF regulates, including Desjardins.

AMF AI guideline effective , May 1, 2027 : 181 days to go

Coming Federal (Canada)

Consumer-Driven Banking Act (open banking)

Royal Assent March 26, 2026, but most provisions are not in force. Regulations were proposed June 27, 2026; final rules are targeted for late 2026 or early 2027. No date yet for data sharing to start.

Proposed Federal (Canada)

Bank anti-fraud regulations (proposed)

Proposed in Canada Gazette Part I on June 27, 2026, with a proposed start date of July 1, 2027. Final version not yet published.

Proposed coming-into-force date , July 1, 2027 : 242 days to go

About the Index

What is the Canadian Bank AI Index? +
An independent scorecard of 35 Canadian banks, credit unions and fintechs, built only from public evidence: where AI reaches customers, how it is governed, whether customers are told, and the talent behind it. Every point links to its source. Read the method .
Which Canadian bank is furthest ahead on AI? +
On public evidence, TD Bank Group (The Toronto-Dominion Bank) scores highest in this edition, with 84 out of 100. See the scorecard for everyone else. Our score measures what institutions disclose and launch, not whether their AI is good or safe. Globally, the Evident AI Index ranks the large banks on talent, innovation, leadership and transparency.
Does my bank use AI on my account? +
Almost certainly for fraud screening, and often for chat, credit and marketing. Our “What AI touches my account?” page lists what each institution has disclosed and explains your rights, including how to ask for a human to review a decision.
Is there an AI bank in Canada? +
Not in the sense of a bank run by AI. Every Canadian bank is run by people and regulated as usual; what differs is how much AI each uses and discloses. Digital banks and fintechs often use AI heavily in onboarding and fraud checks but publish little about it. Compare them on the scorecard .
How often is the Index updated? +
Quarterly, with new deployments added as they are announced. This is the 2026 Q4 edition, with evidence checked to 2026-11-01. The change log lists every update.
